## Limitations

- Small N: 15 prompts (10 text, 5 image), seeds [1, 2], 80 finished runs. Differences smaller than the CIs above can't be told apart from noise.
- Models (50 runs): builder `claude-haiku-5-5`, critic `gemini-3.8-flash`, judge `claude-opus-5-5`.
- Models (30 runs): builder `claude-haiku-5-5`, critic `off`, judge `claude-opus-5-5`.
- The judge shares the builder's model family in 80 runs. Acceptable for E1 and E2: both arms of each comparison use the same builder, so a family preference affects them alike.
- The judge was checked against one person's ratings (E3 above); its pass/fail is part of success, so read the success rates with E3's agreement in mind.
- The answer key grades layout and categories only; looks rest on one judge model.
